E2E Test Check Items
====================

1. Page loads successfully with empty URL input and empty book content area
2. URL bar has BurlyWood background
3. Book content area has wheat background
4. Book content area has line-height 1.4
5. Enter a Gutenberg book URL, press Enter, verify "Loading..." appears
6. After loading, verify book content is displayed in <p> paragraphs
7. After loading, verify paragraphs contain only text (no HTML tags)
8. Each paragraph has a "Convert to Simple English" button
9. Convert buttons have gold background
10. Convert buttons are hidden by default and visible when paragraph is in viewport (IntersectionObserver)
11. Convert buttons are appended inside the paragraph element (not outside)
12. URL is saved to localStorage "book_history" with unique entries
13. On page reload, last URL from book_history is loaded automatically
14. Text selection in book content triggers word meaning modal
15. Word meaning modal is draggable
16. Word meaning modal remembers position in localStorage
17. Word meaning modal has close button that works
18. Word meaning modal body has vertical scrolling (overflow-y: auto)
19. Clicking Convert button opens Simple English modal with wheat background
20. Simple English modal is draggable and remembers position
