E2E Check Items for Gutenberg Book Reader (Puppeteer)
======================================================

1. Page loads without errors
2. URL bar with BurlyWood background exists
3. Book URL input field (id="bookUrl") exists and is visible
4. Book content div (id="bookContent") exists with wheat background
5. Book content div has line-height 1.4
6. Entering a Gutenberg URL and pressing Enter shows "Loading..." text
7. After loading, content is split into paragraphs (<p> elements)
8. Paragraphs contain pure text (no nested HTML tags other than button)
9. Each paragraph contains a "Convert to Simple English" button
10. Convert buttons have gold background
11. Convert buttons are inside the paragraph element (appended at end)
12. Visible paragraphs show the convert button (IntersectionObserver)
13. Non-visible paragraphs hide the convert button
14. Book URL is saved to localStorage "book_history"
15. Entries in "book_history" are unique
16. On reload, the last entry from book_history is loaded into the input and Enter is triggered
17. Modal appears when text is selected in book content
18. Modal is draggable (header has cursor: move)
19. Modal has a close button that removes modal on click
20. Modal body has vertical scrolling (overflow-y: auto)
21. Simple English modal (from Convert button) has wheat background
22. Modal position is saved to localStorage
