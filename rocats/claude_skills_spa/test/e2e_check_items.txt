E2E Check Items for Book Reader SPA (Puppeteer)
=================================================

1. Page loads without errors
2. Body has wheat background
3. Top bar with BurlyWood background exists
4. Top bar uses horizontal layout (flexbox row)
5. Top bar has padding: 10px
6. Book URL input field (id="book_url") exists and is visible
7. Book content div (id="book_content") exists with wheat background
8. Book content div has line-height 1.4
9. Entering a Gutenberg URL and pressing Enter shows "Loading..." text
10. After loading, content is split into paragraphs (<p> elements)
11. Paragraphs contain pure text (no nested HTML tags other than buttons)
12. Each paragraph contains a "Convert to Simple English" button
13. Convert buttons have gold background
14. Convert buttons are inside the paragraph element (appended at end)
15. Visible paragraphs show the convert button (IntersectionObserver)
16. Non-visible paragraphs hide the convert button
17. Book URL is saved to localStorage "book_history"
18. Entries in "book_history" are unique
19. On reload, the last entry from book_history is loaded into the input and content loads
20. Modal appears when text is selected in book content
21. Modal is draggable (header has cursor: move)
22. Modal has a close button that hides modal on click
23. Modal body has vertical scrolling (overflow-y: auto)
24. Modal position is saved to localStorage
25. BookHistory dropdown button exists
26. BookHistory dropdown shows recent URLs when clicked
27. Convert button click shows "Converting..." then replaces paragraph text in-place
28. After conversion, an "Original" button appears in the paragraph
29. Original button has same style as Convert button (gold background)
30. Clicking "Original" button restores original paragraph text
31. BookHistory button has a downward triangle background image
