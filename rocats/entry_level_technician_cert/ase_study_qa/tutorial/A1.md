# ASE A1 - Engine Repair: Overview

Welcome to the ASE A1 Engine Repair study guide! This guide is designed for community college students who are brand new to automotive mechanics. No prior experience is needed. We will walk you through every concept step by step, using plain language and real-world examples so you can build a solid foundation in engine repair.

The A1 certification covers how gasoline engines work, how to diagnose problems, and how to repair or replace engine components. It is one of the most important ASE certifications and a great starting point for your automotive career.

---

## Modules

### [Module 1: General Engine Diagnosis](A1-module-1.md)
Learn how a 4-stroke engine works, get familiar with essential diagnostic tools, and understand how technicians identify engine problems using tests and trouble codes.

### [Module 2: Cylinder Head and Valve Train](A1-module-2.md)
Explore the top end of the engine. Understand what cylinder heads do, how valves open and close, and what happens when head gaskets fail.

### [Module 3: Engine Block Assembly](A1-module-3.md)
Dive into the bottom end of the engine. Learn about pistons, crankshafts, connecting rods, and the procedures used to measure and assemble these critical parts.

### [Module 4: Lubrication and Cooling Systems](A1-module-4.md)
Discover how oil and coolant keep an engine alive. Understand oil circulation, cooling system components, and how to diagnose overheating and oil pressure problems.

---

## How to Use This Guide

Work through the modules in order. Each module builds on the previous one. Read the topic summaries, then test your knowledge with the practice questions in the main ASE study app. Good luck on your journey to becoming a certified technician!
