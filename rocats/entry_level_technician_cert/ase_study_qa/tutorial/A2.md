# ASE A2 - Automatic Transmission/Transaxle

Welcome to the ASE A2 study guide! This certification covers the diagnosis, maintenance, and repair of automatic transmissions and transaxles found in modern vehicles. An automatic transmission is the component that shifts gears for you while driving, transferring engine power to the wheels smoothly and efficiently. Whether a vehicle is front-wheel drive (transaxle) or rear-wheel drive (transmission), the core principles are the same. This guide is designed for beginners with no prior automotive experience.

## Study Modules

### [Module 1: General Transmission Diagnosis](A2-module-1.md)
Learn the fundamentals of how automatic transmissions work, from torque converters to planetary gear sets. You will understand how to inspect transmission fluid, perform road tests, interpret diagnostic trouble codes, and conduct stall speed testing.

### [Module 2: Transmission Maintenance and Adjustment](A2-module-2.md)
Explore routine maintenance tasks like fluid and filter changes, band and linkage adjustments, and cooler inspections. You will also learn about line pressure testing, governor pressure, throttle valve adjustment, and how to diagnose shift quality issues.

### [Module 3: In-Vehicle and Off-Vehicle Repair](A2-module-3.md)
Dive into hands-on repair procedures including seal replacement, valve body service, solenoid replacement, and clutch pack inspection. This module covers both repairs done with the transmission still in the vehicle and those requiring full removal.
