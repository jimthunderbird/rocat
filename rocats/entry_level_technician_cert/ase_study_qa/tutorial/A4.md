# ASE A4 - Suspension and Steering

Welcome to ASE A4! This section covers the systems that let you steer your vehicle and keep it riding smoothly over the road. Suspension and steering are closely connected -- the suspension absorbs bumps and keeps your tires on the ground, while the steering system translates your hand movements on the wheel into turning the front wheels. Understanding these systems is essential for diagnosing handling problems, uneven tire wear, and ride comfort complaints.
