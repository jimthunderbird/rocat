# ASE A5 - Brakes: Overview

Welcome to the ASE A5 Brakes study guide! Brakes are one of the most critical safety systems on any vehicle. Every time a driver presses the brake pedal, a chain of mechanical, hydraulic, and electronic events work together to slow and stop the car. Understanding how brakes work is essential for any automotive technician. This guide is designed for beginners with no prior automotive experience.

The A5 exam covers three major areas, organized into the modules below.

---

## Modules

### [Module 1: Hydraulic System and General Diagnosis](A5-module-1.md)
Learn how hydraulic pressure makes braking possible, the role of brake fluid and the master cylinder, and how to diagnose common brake problems like a spongy or hard pedal.

### [Module 2: Disc and Drum Brakes](A5-module-2.md)
Explore the two main types of friction brakes found on vehicles. You will learn how disc brakes and drum brakes create the friction needed to stop a vehicle, along with their key components and service procedures.

### [Module 3: Power Assist and Electronic Systems](A5-module-3.md)
Discover the systems that make braking easier and safer, including vacuum boosters, anti-lock braking systems (ABS), traction control, electronic stability control, and parking brakes.
