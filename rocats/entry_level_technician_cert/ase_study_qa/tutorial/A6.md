# ASE A6 - Electrical/Electronic Systems

Welcome to the ASE A6 study guide on Electrical/Electronic Systems. This area covers everything that runs on electricity in a vehicle -- from the battery that stores energy, to the starter that cranks the engine, to the lights and accessories that keep you safe and comfortable. Modern cars rely heavily on electrical systems, so understanding how electricity flows through a vehicle is one of the most valuable skills you can build as a technician.

This guide is broken into three modules:

## Modules

### [Module 1: General Electrical Diagnosis and Battery](A6-module-1.md)
Learn the fundamentals of automotive electricity, how to use diagnostic tools like a multimeter, how to read wiring diagrams, and everything about the vehicle battery -- the heart of the electrical system.

### [Module 2: Starting and Charging Systems](A6-module-2.md)
Understand how the starter motor cranks the engine and how the alternator keeps the battery charged while the engine runs. These two systems work together to keep the vehicle running reliably.

### [Module 3: Lighting, Gauges, and Accessories](A6-module-3.md)
Explore the many electrical accessories in a modern vehicle, including headlights, turn signals, instrument gauges, power windows, and the body control module that ties it all together.
