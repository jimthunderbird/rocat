# ASE A7 - Heating and Air Conditioning: Overview

Welcome to the ASE A7 Heating and Air Conditioning study guide! This guide is designed for community college students who are brand new to automotive mechanics. No prior experience is needed. We will walk you through every concept step by step, using plain language and real-world examples so you can build a solid foundation in automotive HVAC systems.

The A7 certification covers how a vehicle's heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems work, how to diagnose problems, and how to service and repair these components. Keeping passengers comfortable in all weather conditions is a critical part of vehicle maintenance, and HVAC skills are in high demand at every shop.

---

## Modules

### [Module 1: A/C System Fundamentals](A7-module-1.md)
Learn how automotive air conditioning works from the ground up. Understand the refrigeration cycle, the different types of refrigerant, and the role of each major component including the compressor, condenser, evaporator, and expansion devices.

### [Module 2: A/C Diagnosis and Service](A7-module-2.md)
Discover how technicians test and troubleshoot A/C systems. Learn to read manifold gauges, detect refrigerant leaks, recover and recharge refrigerant, and diagnose common problems like a failing compressor clutch.

### [Module 3: Heating, Ventilation, and Controls](A7-module-3.md)
Explore the heating side of the HVAC system. Understand how the heater core, blend doors, blower motor, and cabin air filter work together, and learn about automatic temperature controls and the sensors that drive them.

---

## How to Use This Guide

Work through the modules in order. Each module builds on the previous one. Read the topic summaries, then test your knowledge with the practice questions in the main ASE study app. Good luck on your journey to becoming a certified technician!
