# ASE A8 - Engine Performance: Overview

Welcome to the ASE A8 Engine Performance study guide! This guide is designed for community college students who are brand new to automotive mechanics. No prior experience is needed. We will walk you through every concept step by step, using plain language and real-world examples so you can build a solid foundation in engine performance diagnosis and repair.

The A8 certification focuses on how well an engine runs rather than the mechanical parts themselves. You will learn about ignition systems, fuel delivery, emission controls, and the sophisticated computers that manage it all. This is one of the most technology-heavy ASE certifications, and mastering it prepares you for diagnosing the complex driveability problems that modern vehicles present.

---

## Modules

### [Module 1: Ignition and Fuel Systems](A8-module-1.md)
Learn how spark is generated and delivered to each cylinder, how fuel is pressurized and injected, and how the intake system provides clean air. These are the fundamentals of making an engine run.

### [Module 2: Emission Control Systems](A8-module-2.md)
Understand the systems that reduce harmful exhaust pollutants. From catalytic converters to oxygen sensors, discover how vehicles meet environmental standards and how technicians verify these systems are working.

### [Module 3: Computerized Engine Controls](A8-module-3.md)
Explore the electronic brain of the engine. Learn about sensors, actuators, and the engine control module that ties everything together, plus the diagnostic tools and techniques used to analyze computer-controlled systems.

---

## How to Use This Guide

Work through the modules in order. Each module builds on the previous one. Read the topic summaries, then test your knowledge with the practice questions in the main ASE study app. Good luck on your journey to becoming a certified technician!
