# ASE G1 - Auto Maintenance and Light Repair: Overview

Welcome to the ASE G1 Auto Maintenance and Light Repair study guide! This guide is designed for community college students who are brand new to automotive mechanics. No prior experience is needed. We will walk you through every concept step by step, using plain language and real-world examples so you can build a solid foundation in vehicle maintenance and basic repair procedures.

The G1 certification covers the routine maintenance tasks and light repairs that make up the majority of work in quick-service shops and dealership service departments. From oil changes and brake pad replacements to battery testing and tire rotations, these are the skills you will use every single day as an entry-level technician. Mastering G1 material gives you the hands-on confidence to start working on real vehicles right away.

---

## Modules

### [Module 1: Engine and Drivetrain Maintenance](G1-module-1.md)
Learn the essential maintenance procedures that keep engines and drivetrains running smoothly, including oil changes, coolant service, belt inspections, spark plug replacement, and fluid services for transmissions and differentials.

### [Module 2: Brakes, Steering, and Suspension Maintenance](G1-module-2.md)
Discover how to inspect and service the systems that stop, steer, and support a vehicle. From brake pad replacements and rotor inspections to tire rotations and suspension checks, these are critical safety skills.

### [Module 3: Electrical and HVAC Maintenance](G1-module-3.md)
Explore basic electrical and climate control maintenance including battery service, lighting, fuse diagnosis, wiper blades, cabin air filters, A/C inspections, and how to use a multimeter for basic electrical testing.

---

## How to Use This Guide

Work through the modules in order. Each module builds on the previous one. Read the topic summaries, then test your knowledge with the practice questions in the main ASE study app. Good luck on your journey to becoming a certified technician!
