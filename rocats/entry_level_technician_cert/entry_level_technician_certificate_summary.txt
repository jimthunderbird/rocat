SECTION 1: WHAT IS THE 7-UNIT ENTRY LEVEL TECHNICIAN CERTIFICATE?
================================================================================

The 7-unit Entry Level Technician Certificate is a Career Certificate offered by
Skyline College (San Bruno, San Mateo County, CA) through their Automotive
Technology department. Skyline College is nationally certified by the ASE
Education Foundation.

This certificate provides students foundational skills to start an entry-level
job at a repair facility or dealership. Graduates are prepared for positions
such as: Lube Tech, Shop Assistant, Express Tech, Driveway Tech, Porter, Car
Washer, Parts Runner, Detailing, and Customer Service roles.

REQUIRED COURSES (Total: 7 units)
----------------------------------
  Course #    | Title                                          | Units
  ------------|------------------------------------------------|------
  AUTO 580    | Careers in Automotive Technology                | 1
  AUTO 670    | Automotive Work Experience                      | 1
  AUTO 711    | Automotive Entry Level Technician Fundamentals  | 5
  ------------|------------------------------------------------|------
  TOTAL       |                                                | 7

Course Descriptions:

  AUTO 580 (1 unit) - Prepares students for careers in Automotive Technology by
  covering career pathways, soft skills, safety standards, regulation of the
  automotive industry, and labor law. Topics include resume preparation,
  interview skills, workplace expectations, and career options.
  (Lecture: 16-18 hrs/semester; No prerequisites)

  AUTO 670 (1 unit) - Students earn academic credit for employment related to
  their major or career objectives. Students complete an orientation during the
  first three weeks and collaborate with an instructor to establish learning
  goals. One unit of credit is earned for each 48-54 hours of paid or unpaid
  work per semester.
  (Pass/No Pass grading; No prerequisites)

  AUTO 711 (5 units) - Teaches fundamental automotive skills including oil
  changes, safety inspections, fluid flushing, tire service, work order
  preparation, and recommendations.
  (Lecture: 56-63 hrs; Lab: 72-81 hrs; No prerequisites)

Note: AUTO 710 (Fundamentals of Automotive Technology, 4 units) is NOT part of
this certificate but is the prerequisite for the full-time daytime Automotive
Technology AS Degree program.

Source: https://catalog.skylinecollege.edu/current/programs/automotive-technician-entry-level-cc.php

SECTION 2: STEPS TO COMPLETE THE CERTIFICATE
================================================================================

Step 1: ENROLL at Skyline College
  - Apply for admission to Skyline College (part of the San Mateo County
    Community College District - SMCCD)
  - Complete placement assessments if required
  - Meet with an academic counselor

Step 2: COMPLETE the 3 Required Courses (7 units total)
  - AUTO 580: Careers in Automotive Technology (1 unit)
  - AUTO 670: Automotive Work Experience (1 unit)
  - AUTO 711: Automotive Entry Level Technician Fundamentals (5 units)
  - Minimum completion time: approximately 1 semester
  - Courses are offered during both day and evening

Step 3: PREPARE for ASE Entry-Level Certification Exams
  - Study using ASE Study Guides (see Section 4 below)
  - Take practice tests to assess readiness
  - The ASE Entry-Level tests have NO work experience requirement
  - Tests are administered at the school via secure computer-based testing (CBT)

Step 4: TAKE ASE Entry-Level Certification Tests
  - The ASE Automobile Entry-Level series includes 10 tests
  - Tests cost $46-$51 per test
  - Each test has 40-80 scored questions, 120-240 minutes allowed
  - Tests are available year-round and administered online at the school
  - A proctor (staff person other than an automotive instructor) monitors tests
  - No work experience is required to sit for entry-level tests

Step 5: EARN Entry-Level Certifications
  - Pass one or more tests to earn entry-level certification
  - Entry-level certifications are valid for 2 years (non-renewable)
  - Certificates are printed and signed by school principals
  - Digital badges are provided upon completion

Step 6: PROGRESS to Professional ASE Certification (Optional Future Step)
  - After gaining 2 years of hands-on work experience, you can take the
    professional ASE A1-A8 tests
  - Professional certifications are renewable (retest every 5 years)
  - Passing all A1-A8 professional tests earns Master Automobile Technician
    status

SECTION 3: ASE ENTRY-LEVEL AUTOMOBILE TESTS (10 Tests)
================================================================================

The ASE Entry-Level Automobile series includes these 10 tests:

  #  | Entry-Level Test Name              | Maps to Professional Test
  ---|------------------------------------|--------------------------
  1  | Engine Repair                      | A1
  2  | Automatic Transmission/Transaxle   | A2
  3  | Manual Drive Train and Axles       | A3
  4  | Suspension and Steering            | A4
  5  | Brakes                             | A5
  6  | Electrical/Electronic Systems      | A6
  7  | Heating and Air Conditioning       | A7
  8  | Engine Performance                 | A8
  9  | Automobile Service Technology      | (General/Comprehensive)
  10 | Maintenance and Light Repair       | G1
  ---|------------------------------------|--------------------------

Key Differences from Professional A1-A8 Tests:
  - NO work experience required (professional tests require 2 years)
  - Curriculum-based (built around ASE Education Foundation task lists)
  - Valid for 2 years only (non-renewable)
  - Administered at schools, not at independent testing centers
  - Designed for students near the end of their studies

SECTION 4: ANSWERS TO YOUR QUESTIONS
================================================================================

QUESTION 1: Are the subjects all in the ase_study_guides?
--------------------------------------------------------------
YES. All 10 ASE Entry-Level Automobile test subjects are covered by the ASE
Study Guides:

  - Tests 1-8 (Engine Repair through Engine Performance) map directly to the
    "Automobile & Light Truck (Includes Exhaust Systems) (A1 - A9)" study guide
    >> File: ASE_Automobile_Study_Guide_2026.pdf

  - Test 10 (Maintenance and Light Repair) maps to the
    "Auto Maintenance & Light Repair (G1)" study guide
    >> File: ASE_MaintenanceLtRepair_Study_Guide_2026.pdf

  - Test 9 (Automobile Service Technology) is a general/comprehensive test
    that draws from the A1-A8 content areas, so it is also covered by the
    Automobile & Light Truck study guide.

  - The "ASE Certification: Need to Know" guide provides general test-taking
    information applicable to all tests.
    >> File: ASE_CertificationNeedtoKnow_2026.pdf


QUESTION 2: Do I need to study the entire ase_study_guides?
--------------------------------------------------------------
NO. You do NOT need to study the entire collection of ASE Study Guides. The
full set of ASE Study Guides covers 22 topics across many specialties (transit
bus, collision repair, medium-heavy truck, school bus, military vehicles, parts
specialist, CNG, service consultant, and advanced levels L1-L4). Most of these
are NOT relevant to the Entry-Level Technician certificate.

You only need to focus on 3 out of the 22 study guides.


QUESTION 3: What are the specific topics that are covered?
--------------------------------------------------------------
You need to study the following ASE Study Guide topics:

  REQUIRED STUDY GUIDES:
  ----------------------

  1. "ASE Certification: Need to Know"
     >> General test information, registration, test-taking strategies
     >> File: ASE_CertificationNeedtoKnow_2026.pdf

  2. "Automobile & Light Truck (Includes Exhaust Systems) (A1 - A9)"
     >> This is the PRIMARY study guide. It covers:

     A1 - Engine Repair (45 scored questions, 75 min)
       - General Engine Diagnosis (40%)
       - Cylinder Head and Valve Train Diagnosis and Repair (13%)
       - Engine Block Diagnosis and Repair (9%)
       - Lubrication and Cooling Systems Diagnosis and Repair (18%)
       - Fuel, Electrical, Ignition, Air Induction and Exhaust Systems (20%)

     A2 - Automatic Transmission/Transaxle (45 scored questions, 75 min)
       - Transmission/Transaxle Diagnosis (58%)
       - In-Vehicle Maintenance and Repair (27%)
       - Unit Removal, Inspection, and Installation (15%)

     A3 - Manual Drive Train and Axles (40 scored questions, 60 min)
       - Clutch Diagnosis and Repair (16%)
       - Transmission Diagnosis and Repair (17%)
       - Transaxle Diagnosis and Repair (17%)
       - Drive Shaft/Half-Shaft and Universal/CV Joint (13%)
       - Drive Axle Diagnosis and Repair (17%)
       - Four-Wheel/All-Wheel Drive Component Diagnosis (20%)

     A4 - Suspension and Steering (40 scored questions, 60 min)
       - Steering Systems Diagnosis and Repair (30%)
       - Suspension Systems Diagnosis and Repair (30%)
       - Wheel Alignment Diagnosis, Adjustment, and Repair (28%)
       - Wheel and Tire Diagnosis and Service (12%)

     A5 - Brakes (45 scored questions, 75 min)
       - Hydraulic, Power Assist, and Parking Brake Systems (43%)
       - Drum Brake Diagnosis and Repair (11%)
       - Disc Brake Diagnosis and Repair (24%)
       - Electronic Brake Control Systems: ABS, TCS, ESC (22%)

     A6 - Electrical/Electronic Systems (50 scored questions, 90 min)
       - General Electrical/Electronic System Diagnosis (26%)
       - Battery and Starting System Diagnosis and Repair (18%)
       - Charging System Diagnosis and Repair (10%)
       - Lighting Systems Diagnosis and Repair (12%)
       - Body Electrical Systems Diagnosis and Repair (34%)

     A7 - Heating and Air Conditioning (50 scored questions, 75 min)
       - HVAC and Engine Cooling System Service/Diagnosis/Repair (42%)
       - Refrigeration System Component Diagnosis and Repair (20%)
       - Operating Systems and Related Controls Diagnosis/Repair (38%)

     A8 - Engine Performance (50 scored questions, 75 min)
       - General Diagnosis (24%)
       - Ignition System Diagnosis and Repair (16%)
       - Fuel, Air Induction and Exhaust Systems (18%)
       - Emissions Control Systems Diagnosis and Repair (16%)
       - Computerized Engine Controls Diagnosis and Repair (26%)

     >> File: ASE_Automobile_Study_Guide_2026.pdf

  3. "Auto Maintenance & Light Repair (G1)"
     >> Covers the Maintenance and Light Repair entry-level test
     >> File: ASE_MaintenanceLtRepair_Study_Guide_2026.pdf


  STUDY GUIDES YOU DO NOT NEED:
  -----------------------------
  - Collision Repair & Refinishing (B2-B6)
  - Transit Bus (H1-H8)
  - Advanced Level L1, L2, L3, L4
  - Parts Specialist (P1, P2)
  - Medium-Heavy Truck (T1-T8)
  - School Bus (S1-S7)
  - Military Tactical Wheeled Vehicle (MIL Series)
  - Military Fundamentals (MIL 1)
  - CNG Vehicle (F1)
  - Service Consultant (C1)

  Note: Within the Automobile & Light Truck study guide, A9 (Light Vehicle
  Diesel Engines) is also NOT required for the Entry-Level Technician
  certificate or for Master Automobile Technician status.
