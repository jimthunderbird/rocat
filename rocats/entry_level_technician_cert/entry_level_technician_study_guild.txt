2. "Automobile & Light Truck (Includes Exhaust Systems) (A1 - A9)"
   >> This is the PRIMARY study guide. It covers:

   A1 - Engine Repair (45 scored questions, 75 min)
     - General Engine Diagnosis (40%)
     - Cylinder Head and Valve Train Diagnosis and Repair (13%)
     - Engine Block Diagnosis and Repair (9%)
     - Lubrication and Cooling Systems Diagnosis and Repair (18%)
     - Fuel, Electrical, Ignition, Air Induction and Exhaust Systems (20%)

   A2 - Automatic Transmission/Transaxle (45 scored questions, 75 min)
     - Transmission/Transaxle Diagnosis (58%)
     - In-Vehicle Maintenance and Repair (27%)
     - Unit Removal, Inspection, and Installation (15%)

   A3 - Manual Drive Train and Axles (40 scored questions, 60 min)
     - Clutch Diagnosis and Repair (16%)
     - Transmission Diagnosis and Repair (17%)
     - Transaxle Diagnosis and Repair (17%)
     - Drive Shaft/Half-Shaft and Universal/CV Joint (13%)
     - Drive Axle Diagnosis and Repair (17%)
     - Four-Wheel/All-Wheel Drive Component Diagnosis (20%)

   A4 - Suspension and Steering (40 scored questions, 60 min)
     - Steering Systems Diagnosis and Repair (30%)
     - Suspension Systems Diagnosis and Repair (30%)
     - Wheel Alignment Diagnosis, Adjustment, and Repair (28%)
     - Wheel and Tire Diagnosis and Service (12%)

   A5 - Brakes (45 scored questions, 75 min)
     - Hydraulic, Power Assist, and Parking Brake Systems (43%)
     - Drum Brake Diagnosis and Repair (11%)
     - Disc Brake Diagnosis and Repair (24%)
     - Electronic Brake Control Systems: ABS, TCS, ESC (22%)

   A6 - Electrical/Electronic Systems (50 scored questions, 90 min)
     - General Electrical/Electronic System Diagnosis (26%)
     - Battery and Starting System Diagnosis and Repair (18%)
     - Charging System Diagnosis and Repair (10%)
     - Lighting Systems Diagnosis and Repair (12%)
     - Body Electrical Systems Diagnosis and Repair (34%)

   A7 - Heating and Air Conditioning (50 scored questions, 75 min)
     - HVAC and Engine Cooling System Service/Diagnosis/Repair (42%)
     - Refrigeration System Component Diagnosis and Repair (20%)
     - Operating Systems and Related Controls Diagnosis/Repair (38%)

   A8 - Engine Performance (50 scored questions, 75 min)
     - General Diagnosis (24%)
     - Ignition System Diagnosis and Repair (16%)
     - Fuel, Air Induction and Exhaust Systems (18%)
     - Emissions Control Systems Diagnosis and Repair (16%)
     - Computerized Engine Controls Diagnosis and Repair (26%)

   >> File: ASE_Automobile_Study_Guide_2026.pdf
