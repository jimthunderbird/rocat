# AI Foundations

This section covers the introductory AI courses in the UCSD BS AI program: **CSE 55** (Theory of Computation), **CSE 150A** (Introduction to AI: Search and Reasoning), and **CSE 150B** (Introduction to AI: Probabilistic Reasoning and Learning). These courses introduce the classical ideas that launched the field of artificial intelligence and remain central to modern AI systems.

## What You Will Learn

Artificial intelligence is the study of building agents that act intelligently -- perceiving their environment, reasoning about it, and taking actions to achieve goals. In these courses you will explore three pillars of AI: search and planning, logical reasoning, and probabilistic inference. Each represents a different way of modeling and solving problems.

## Module Breakdown

- **[Module 1 -- Search & Planning](AIF-module-1.md)**
  Learn how AI agents explore possibilities to find optimal solutions, from simple graph search to informed heuristic methods like A*.

- **[Module 2 -- Logic & Reasoning](AIF-module-2.md)**
  Discover how AI systems represent knowledge formally and draw conclusions using propositional and first-order logic.

- **[Module 3 -- Probabilistic AI](AIF-module-3.md)**
  Understand how AI handles uncertainty through probability, Bayes' theorem, and graphical models like Bayesian networks.

## Why It Matters for AI

- **Search algorithms** power game-playing AI, route planning, and puzzle solving.
- **Logical reasoning** is the basis of expert systems, automated theorem provers, and constraint satisfaction.
- **Probabilistic models** underlie speech recognition, medical diagnosis, and every system that must make decisions under uncertainty.

These classical foundations complement the data-driven approaches of machine learning. The best AI systems combine both paradigms.

## Tips for Success

- Think of AI problems in terms of states, actions, and goals.
- Draw out search trees and probability diagrams by hand.
- Implement the algorithms yourself to build intuition beyond the theory.
