# Computer Vision

This section covers the computer vision courses in the UCSD BS AI program: **CSE 106** (Computer Graphics), **CSE 152A** (Introduction to Computer Vision), and **CSE 152B** (Advanced Computer Vision). These courses teach machines to interpret and understand visual information from the world -- images, videos, and 3D scenes.

## What You Will Learn

**Computer vision** is the field of AI that enables computers to extract meaningful information from visual data. You will learn how images are represented digitally, how classical algorithms detect edges and features, and how modern deep learning architectures achieve human-level performance on tasks like object detection and image segmentation.

## Module Breakdown

- **[Module 1 -- Image Processing Basics](CV-module-1.md)**
  Understand how digital images are represented and manipulated, including filtering, edge detection, and transformations.

- **[Module 2 -- Feature Extraction & Object Detection](CV-module-2.md)**
  Learn how to identify distinctive features in images and use them for matching, recognition, and locating objects.

- **[Module 3 -- Deep Learning for Vision](CV-module-3.md)**
  Explore convolutional neural networks and modern architectures that power image classification, segmentation, and generation.

## Why It Matters for AI

- **Self-driving cars** rely on vision to detect lanes, pedestrians, and traffic signs.
- **Medical imaging** uses computer vision to detect tumors and anomalies in scans.
- **Augmented reality** blends virtual objects with the real world using visual understanding.
- **Robotics** depends on vision for navigation and manipulation.

Computer vision is one of the most impactful and rapidly advancing areas of AI, with new breakthroughs emerging regularly.

## Tips for Success

- Experiment with real images using OpenCV and PyTorch.
- Build intuition by visualizing intermediate results (filters, feature maps, activations).
- Study both the classical and deep learning approaches -- they complement each other.
