# Data Structures & Algorithms

This section covers the core algorithm and data structure courses in the UCSD BS AI program: **CSE 100** (Advanced Data Structures), **CSE 101** (Design and Analysis of Algorithms), and **CSE 103** (A Probabilistic Approach to Algorithms). These courses teach you how to organize data efficiently and solve computational problems within practical time and space constraints.

## What You Will Learn

A **data structure** is a way of organizing data so that it can be accessed and modified efficiently. An **algorithm** is a step-by-step procedure for solving a problem. Together, they determine whether your program runs in milliseconds or hours. You will learn to choose the right structure for each situation and to prove that your algorithms are correct and efficient.

## Module Breakdown

- **[Module 1 -- Trees & Graphs](DSA-module-1.md)**
  Explore hierarchical and network data structures including binary search trees, heaps, and graph representations that power everything from databases to social networks.

- **[Module 2 -- Sorting & Searching](DSA-module-2.md)**
  Study the classic sorting algorithms, understand their time complexities, and learn efficient search techniques including hashing.

- **[Module 3 -- Dynamic Programming & Greedy Algorithms](DSA-module-3.md)**
  Master two powerful algorithm design paradigms for optimization problems that appear throughout AI and systems.

## Why It Matters for AI

- **Search algorithms** are the backbone of game-playing AI and planning systems.
- **Graph algorithms** power knowledge graphs, recommendation engines, and network analysis.
- **Dynamic programming** appears in sequence alignment (NLP), reinforcement learning, and probabilistic inference.
- Efficient data structures make the difference between an AI prototype and a production system.

## Tips for Success

- Trace algorithms by hand on small examples before coding.
- Always analyze time and space complexity using big-O notation.
- Practice on platforms like LeetCode and HackerRank to build fluency.
