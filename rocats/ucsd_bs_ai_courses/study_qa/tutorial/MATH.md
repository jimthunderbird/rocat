# Mathematics for CS

This section covers the essential mathematics courses in the UCSD BS AI program: **MATH 20A-C** (Calculus), **MATH 18** (Linear Algebra), **CSE 20** (Discrete Mathematics), and **CSE 21** (Mathematics for Algorithms and Systems). These courses provide the formal language and tools that underpin every area of artificial intelligence.

## What You Will Learn

Mathematics is the language in which AI is written. Calculus tells you how functions change, which is central to training neural networks. Linear algebra provides the framework for representing data as vectors and matrices. Discrete math gives you the logical reasoning and counting techniques needed to analyze algorithms and prove correctness.

## Module Breakdown

- **[Module 1 -- Calculus Essentials](MATH-module-1.md)**
  Understand derivatives, integrals, and multivariable calculus -- the machinery behind optimization and gradient descent.

- **[Module 2 -- Linear Algebra](MATH-module-2.md)**
  Learn how vectors, matrices, and transformations represent data and computation in machine learning and computer graphics.

- **[Module 3 -- Discrete Math & Combinatorics](MATH-module-3.md)**
  Explore logic, proof techniques, sets, counting, and graph theory -- the toolkit for analyzing algorithms and modeling discrete systems.

## Why It Matters for AI

- **Gradient descent**, the core algorithm for training neural networks, relies on multivariable calculus.
- **Dimensionality reduction** techniques like PCA are pure linear algebra.
- **Algorithm analysis** (big-O notation, recurrences) comes from discrete math.
- **Probability** (covered more deeply in later courses) builds on calculus and combinatorics.

## Tips for Success

- Work through problems by hand before checking solutions; mathematical maturity comes from struggle.
- Connect each topic to a CS application so the material feels concrete.
- Form study groups -- explaining a concept to someone else is the best way to solidify your understanding.
