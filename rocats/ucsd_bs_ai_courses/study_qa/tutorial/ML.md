# Machine Learning

This section covers the machine learning courses in the UCSD BS AI program: **CSE 151A** (Introduction to Machine Learning), **CSE 151B** (Deep Learning), and **CSE 156** (Statistical Natural Language Processing). These courses teach you how to build systems that learn patterns from data rather than being explicitly programmed.

## What You Will Learn

**Machine learning (ML)** is the science of getting computers to act without being explicitly programmed for every scenario. Instead of writing rules by hand, you provide data and an algorithm that discovers the rules automatically. You will study the major families of ML algorithms, learn how to train and evaluate models, and understand the mathematical principles that make learning possible.

## Module Breakdown

- **[Module 1 -- Supervised Learning](ML-module-1.md)**
  Explore algorithms that learn from labeled examples, including linear regression, logistic regression, decision trees, and support vector machines.

- **[Module 2 -- Neural Networks & Deep Learning](ML-module-2.md)**
  Understand how neural networks learn hierarchical representations, from simple perceptrons to convolutional and recurrent architectures.

- **[Module 3 -- Model Evaluation & Regularization](ML-module-3.md)**
  Learn how to measure model performance, avoid overfitting, and select the best model using cross-validation and regularization techniques.

## Why It Matters for AI

Machine learning is the engine that powers modern AI. Every time a voice assistant understands your speech, a recommendation system suggests a movie, or a self-driving car recognizes a pedestrian, machine learning is at work. Mastering these techniques is the core skill of an AI practitioner.

## Tips for Success

- Implement algorithms from scratch at least once to understand the math.
- Use libraries like scikit-learn and PyTorch for real projects.
- Always split your data into training, validation, and test sets.
- Visualize your data and results at every stage.
